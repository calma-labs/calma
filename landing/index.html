How it works

2 mechanisms, 1 outcome:
capital working harder.

M1

Withdraw Queue

The problem

When everyone wants their money out at once, lending breaks. Lenders panic, rates spike, systems crisis.

How Calma fixes it

Instead of race conditions, we use a queue. You request a withdrawal, it processes in order. Predictable. Fair. No front-running.

M2

Dynamic Curve

The problem

Static interest curves don't adapt to real demand. Utilization spikes, but rates stay the same. Capital locks in inefficient markets.

How Calma fixes it

Our curve moves in real-time. More demand → rates rise smoothly. Less demand → rates drop. Supply and demand, always balanced.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Not finding what you need? Email us.

When is mainnet?

Summer 2026 or later. Traction-driven, not timeline-driven. Mainnet launches when product is stable and audited, testnet shows real demand and meaningful TVL, and the community is ready. Rushing to mainnet with weak traction = doomed protocol.

Is Calma safe?

Testnet is for testing — only use money you can afford to lose. Professional security audits will run before mainnet. All findings will be public. If critical issues arise, we fix them before launch — no matter the timeline.

How does the referral program work?

Unique referral link in your profile. To access closed beta: invite 5+ people OR hit $1M in lending/borrowing volume. Whichever comes first unlocks your beta access. All tracked on-chain.

What if I lose my seed phrase?

This is testnet. Points are non-custodial (stored on-chain), but if you lose access to your wallet, we cannot recover them. Use a wallet you're comfortable with.

Will there be a token?

We're exploring tokenomics, but nothing is decided. Once mainnet launches, governance will determine token mechanics, supply, and how it relates to your testnet points. No guarantees on 1:1 conversion or anything else.

How can I get involved beyond testnet?

Join our Telegram and follow us on X for updates. Reach out at info.calmaprotocol@gmail.com if you're an institution, integrator, or researcher.
